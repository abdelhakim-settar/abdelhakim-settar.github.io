Scientific profile

My research focuses on thermochemical transformations and coupled transport. My contributions connect catalytic methane steam reforming with thermal degradation of composite and bio-based materials through the study of kinetics, flow, and heat and mass transfer. The approach combines experimental characterisation, parameter identification and numerical modelling.
