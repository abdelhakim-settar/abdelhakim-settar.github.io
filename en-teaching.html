Current activities

Life cycle assessment

Since 2026, I have coordinated life cycle assessment (LCA) teaching for BUT years 1, 2 and 3 in the Civil Engineering and Sustainable Construction department, bringing environmental assessment into construction education.

Nuclear Passport

I coordinate the Nuclear Passport initiative in the GCCD department under the internal objectives and resources agreement (CIOM), contributing to education and career awareness in the nuclear sector.

Thermal sciences & energy

At INSA Centre Val de Loire, I coordinated the thermal sciences and energy module for the STPI, MRI and ERE departments from 2020 to 2026. My teaching experience includes:

  • Heat transfer and thermal science
  • Fluid mechanics
  • Energy systems
  • Physics and materials

Lectures, tutorials, assessment and teaching coordination in heat transfer, fluid mechanics, energy materials, optics and waves. Other teaching has covered materials physics and chemistry, geothermal energy, energy networks, air treatment and electromagnetism. I have also supervised introductory research projects and apprentices.
