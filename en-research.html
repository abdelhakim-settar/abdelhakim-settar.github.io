01

Hydrogen & biomass

I study thermochemical pathways to hydrogen, including methane steam reforming, biomass pyrolysis and gasification. Kinetic and thermogravimetric analyses help characterise decomposition mechanisms and inform modelling.

02

Transport & porous media

My work investigates heat and mass transfer in porous media and reactive systems. It combines permeability measurements, flow modelling and CFD, particularly with OpenFOAM, to study catalytic reactors and multiphase systems.

03

Materials & characterisation

I investigate the thermal characterisation of bio-based materials and biocomposites. Experiments explore relationships between composition, degradation and thermal properties, with applications in energy and thermal protection.

Sustainable development

Goals that give direction to my work.

My research and teaching address themes connected to these United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

SDG 7Affordable and clean energy

Hydrogen production, thermochemical biomass conversion and improved transport in energy systems.

SDG 9Industry, innovation and infrastructure

Reactor intensification, multiphysics modelling and artificial intelligence for process design and optimisation.

SDG 12Responsible consumption and production

Biomass waste valorisation, bio-based materials and teaching in life cycle assessment.

SDG 13Climate action

Research on energy production pathways and process efficiency, with attention to decarbonisation challenges.

SDG 4Quality education

Education in energy engineering and sustainable construction, project-based learning and supervision of master’s and doctoral research.
