Research · Abdelhakim Settar

Research & projects

From experiments to simulation, my work addresses coupled transport, fluid flow and material transformations, with a particular focus on hydrogen production.

Hydrogen & biomassTransport & porous mediaMaterials & characterisationMethods & toolsProjectsSupervision
01

Hydrogen & biomass

I study thermochemical pathways to hydrogen, including methane steam reforming, biomass pyrolysis and gasification. Kinetic and thermogravimetric analyses help characterise decomposition mechanisms and inform modelling.

02

Transport & porous media

My work investigates heat and mass transfer in porous media and reactive systems. It combines permeability measurements, flow modelling and CFD, particularly with OpenFOAM, to study catalytic reactors and multiphase systems.

03

Materials & characterisation

I investigate the thermal characterisation of bio-based materials and biocomposites. Experiments explore relationships between composition, degradation and thermal properties, with applications in energy and thermal protection.
